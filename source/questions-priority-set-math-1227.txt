H1: 1  VECTOR ANALYSIS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

── 1.1  Vectors: Linear Dependence / Independence ──────────────────────────────

(1)  Determine whether the vectors a = 2i + j − 3k, b = i − 4k, c = 4i + 3j − k
     are linearly dependent or independent. If dependent, find a relation between
     them and show that their terminal points are collinear.
     (4–5 Marks)  [2014-Q1(a), 2015-Q4(a), 2019-Q1(a)]

(2)  Determine whether the vectors r₁ = 2i − j + k,  r₂ = i + 3j − 2k,
     r₃ = −2i + j − 3k,  r₄ = 3i + 2j + 5k  are linearly dependent or
     independent.
     (3–4 Marks)  [2020-Q1(a), 2021-Q1(a)]

(4)  Determine the volume of a tetrahedron whose adjacent edges are
     A = 2i + j − 3k,  B = i − 4k,  C = 4i + 3j − k.
     (4 Marks)  [2020-Q1(b)]

── 1.2  Curves, Tangent Vectors & Curvature ────────────────────────────────────

(5)  Find the unit tangent vector at the point where t = 2 on the curve
     x = t² + 1,  y = 4t − 3,  z = 2t² − 6t.
     (3–4 Marks)  [2019-Q1(b), 2023-Q1(b)]

(6)  Find the curvature k for the curve  x = t,  y = t²,  z = 2t³/3.
     (3 Marks)  [2023-Q1(c)]

── 1.3  Gradient, Divergence, Curl ─────────────────────────────────────────────

(8)  What is the physical significance of ∇·∇φ (Laplacian)?
     (4 Marks)  [2014-Q2(a)]

(9)  Write down the geometrical interpretation of ∇Φ.
     (5 Marks)  [2023-Q2(a)]

(10) Find the angles which the vector F = (y²x − z)i + (yz² + x)j − 2x²z²k
     makes with the coordinate axes. Also find ∇(∇·F) at the point (2, −1, 0).
     (4 Marks)  [2014-Q1(b), 2014-Q1(c)]

(12) If V = (x + 3y)i + (y − 2z)j + (x + az)k, determine the constant 'a' so
     that the divergence of V is zero.
     (4 Marks)  [2016-Q3(c)]

(13) Find the constants a, b, c so that
     V = (x + 2y + az)i + (bx − 3y − z)j + (4x + cy + 2z)k  is irrotational.
     (3 Marks)  [2015-Q4(c)]

(14) Prove that:  (i) ∇ × (∇φ) = 0   (ii) ∇·(∇ × A) = 0.
     (4 Marks)  [2018-Q3(b)]

── 1.4  Directional Derivative ─────────────────────────────────────────────────

(15) Find the directional derivative of  φ = x²yz + 4xz²  at (1, −2, −1)
     in the direction  2i − j − 2k.
     (4 Marks)  [2016-Q3(b), 2017-Q3(b)]

(16) Find the directional derivative of  φ = 4xz³ − 3x²y²z  at (2, −1, 2)
     in the direction  2i − 3j + 6k.
     (3 Marks)  [2021-Q1(b)]

(17) In what direction from the point (1, 3, 2) is the directional derivative of
     φ = 2xz − y²  a maximum? What is the magnitude of this maximum?
     (3 Marks)  [2022-Q1(b)]

── 1.5  Conservative Fields & Scalar Potential ─────────────────────────────────

(19) Show that A = (6xy + z³)i + (3x² − z)j + (3xz² − y)k is irrotational.
     Find φ such that A = ∇φ.
     (4 Marks)  [2017-Q3(a)]

(20) Show that F = (2xy + z²)i + x²j + 3xz²k is a conservative force field
     and find the scalar potential.
     (4–6 Marks)  [2020-Q2(a), 2022-Q1(c)]

(21) Show that F = e^x cos z i + 2yj − e^x sin z k is irrotational. Find scalar
     function φ such that F = ∇φ.
     (4 Marks)  [2020-Q1(c)]

(22) Show that F = xi + yj + zk is a conservative force field. Find scalar
     potential Ø such that F = ∇Ø.
     (4 Marks)  [2021-Q1(c)]

── 1.6  Line Integrals ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

(28) If F = 3xy i − y² j, evaluate ∫_C F·dr where C is the curve y = 2x²
     from (0, 0) to (1, 2).
     (4 Marks)  [2014-Q2(b)]

(30) If F = (2x + y)i + (3y − x)j, evaluate ∫_C F·dr where C is the curve in
     the xy-plane consisting of straight lines from (0,0) to (2,0) and then
     to (3,2).
     (5 Marks)  [2022-Q2(a)]

(31) Evaluate ∫_C A·dr along the curve x² + y² = 1, z = 1, in the positive
     direction from (0,1,1) to (1,0,1) if
     A = (yz + zx)i + xzj + (xy + 2z)k.
     (4–5 Marks)  [2023-Q2(b), 2024-Q1(c)]

── 1.7  Surface Integrals ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────

(34) Evaluate ∬_S F·n̂ dS for F = xi + 2yj − zk where S is the surface of
     the plane 2x + y = 6 in the first octant cut off by z = 4.
     (4 Marks)  [2014-Q2(c)]

(36) If F = 4xzi − y²j + yzk, evaluate ∬_S F·n̂ dS where S is the surface
     of the cube bounded by x=0, x=1, y=0, y=1, z=0, z=1.
     (5–6 Marks)  [2016-Q4(b), 2018-Q4(a), 2020-Q2(b)]

── 1.8  Volume Integrals ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

(41) If F = (2x² − 3z)i − 2xyj − 4xk, evaluate ∭_V (∇ × F) dv where V is
     the closed region bounded by x=0, y=0, z=0 and 2x+2y+z=4.
     (5–6 Marks)  [2019-Q2(a), 2024-Q2(b)]

── 1.9  Green's Theorem ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

(43) Verify Green's theorem for ∮{(xy − x²)dx + x²y dy} where C is the
     perimeter of the triangle bounded by x=1, y=0 and y=x.
     (6 Marks)  [2016-Q4(a)]

── 1.10  Stokes' Theorem ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────

(47) Verify Stokes' theorem for A = 3yi − xzj + yz²k where s is the surface
     of the paraboloid 2z = x² + y² bounded by z = 2 with c traversed
     clockwise.
     (5 Marks)  [2023-Q3(b)]

── 1.11  Divergence Theorem ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────

(50) State and prove the Divergence theorem.
     (5–6 Marks)  [2020-Q3(a), 2021-Q3(a), 2022-Q3(a), 2024-Q3(a)]

(51) Apply the Divergence theorem to compute ∬_S u·n̂ ds where s is the
     surface of the cylinder x² + y² = a² bounded by z=0, z=b and
     u = xi − yj + zk.
     (6 Marks)  [2017-Q4(a)]

── 3.2  Exact Differential Equations ───────────────────────────────────────────

(8)  Define exact differential equation. Show that the necessary condition for

     (12y + 4x³ + 6x²)dx + 3(x + xy²)dy = 0.
